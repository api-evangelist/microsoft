The fourth article in the Agent Experience series examines how multiple extensions compete for limited context window space, identifying three conflict types: vocabulary collisions, guidance conflicts, and resource competition. It argues that past a threshold each additional extension reduces overall performance, and testing should measure net lift across the full stack rather than individual extension impact.
