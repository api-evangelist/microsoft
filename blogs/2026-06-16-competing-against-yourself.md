Addresses the challenge of transitioning from established tools to newer replacements, noting that training data imbalance leads AI models to keep selecting deprecated versions. Recommends shipping agent extensions on day one, making deprecations machine-readable, choosing distinctive names, and publishing migration-focused content to influence future model training.
